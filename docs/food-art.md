# Food art v2

Generated with built-in ImageGen, using the user-supplied `78-Food_Sprites_2.webp` as a quality/shading reference only. Original output: `dist/assets/food-atlas-v2.png`. Twelve isolated sprites were extracted by alpha connected components, preserving original pixels and transparency. No reference-sheet sprites are shipped.

Prompt: Transparent 4-column, 3-row atlas of Malaysian nasi kandar food portions. Detailed hand-painted cartoon game sprites, crisp dark-brown outlines, warm colors, oblique overhead perspective, top-left lighting, generous padding, no labels or plates. Row 1: basmati rice, crispy bone-in chicken thigh/drumstick, broad boneless fried breast, turmeric cabbage. Row 2: ridged green okra, red sambal squid rings/tentacles, salted egg halves, curry beef chunks. Row 3: whole fried mackerel, orange fish curry, brown beef curry, golden lentil dhal. Strong silhouettes and appetizing ingredient texture rather than basic geometric shapes.

Used for counter portions, order icons, dragging and plated food in both rendering modes. Sauce illustration output includes curry ingredients. Browser visual QA covered Canvas compatibility mode; hardware WebGL remains unverified in this environment.
